How it works

From first call to first kilowatt in about 6 weeks

  1. 1

    Free roof assessment

    We review satellite imagery and your last 12 months of bills, then visit to measure the roof and check the electrical panel.

    Custom design

FAQ

Questions we hear every week

How much does a home solar system cost?+

Most homes we install are between 6 and 11 kW, typically $17,000–$32,000 before incentives. Your exact price depends on roof layout, equipment and whether you add a battery. Use the calculator above for a starting estimate, and we'll firm it up for free.

Will solar work during a power outage?+

Grid-tied panels shut off during an outage for line-worker safety. Add a battery and your essential circuits keep running, and the panels recharge the battery each day.

How long do panels last?+

Our tier-1 panels carry a 25-year performance warranty and typically produce more than 85% of their original output after 25 years. Inverters are warranted for 12–25 years, depending on the model.

Do I need a new roof first?+

If your roof has less than about 8 years of life left, re-roofing first usually saves money. We'll tell you honestly during the assessment, and we can coordinate with a roofer.

Can I finance my system?+

Yes: cash, 10–20 year solar loans, or a lease. We'll show the 25-year cost of each side by side so you can choose.
